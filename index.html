Find your itch →Why: four quick questions turn a vague frustration into a clear problem statement. About 30 seconds.
01

Find the itch.

Start from a frustration you have seen up close, not a trend. It should be repeated, costly and annoying enough that people already complain.

02

Understand the problem.

Who exactly has it? How often? What do they do today, and what does it cost them in time, money and mistakes?

03

Discover the opportunity.

Look at what existing products miss. A gap is real only if the people in it are underserved and reachable.

04

Test the evidence.

Talk to 10–15 real people. Ask about the past, not the future. A payment is worth more than a compliment.

05

Build what matters.

Score ideas on seven weighted criteria, drop weak ones early, and build only what the evidence supports.
